# Changelog

## 1.0.6 (Windows and macOS)

- **New name in your DAW.** The plug-in is now listed as **MCA** by **Isoda**, in the *Analyzer* category. Projects saved with earlier versions still open it, with all settings intact.
- **Easier to read.** The tagline and copyright line at the bottom of the window are larger.
- **Opens at full size.** A new plug-in window now opens at its full size instead of 70 %, and the Simple and Advanced views each remember their own size.
- **Manual-install packages** for Windows and macOS, each with a step-by-step install guide, the Quick Start guide and the license.

## 1.0.5

- The Stereo Field explains itself when you hover over it: the display zooms to fit the signal, so its size shows the shape of the stereo image, not the loudness.
- First macOS version for Apple silicon, as VST3 and Audio Unit.

## 1.0.4

- Dark navy design with teal for Stereo, amber for Mono, pink for Side and blue for Left/Right.
- Guidance for pulling narration out of a video's stereo audio.

## 1.0.3

- Redesigned faceplate: flat segmented switches, LED-ladder output meters and a 0 to −9 dB scale for the mono fold-down loss.
- Freeware license with a privacy section: MCA collects no data and makes no network connections.

## 1.0.2

- MIDI Learn reads your controller directly, so no MIDI routing is needed.
- A new instance starts in Stereo.

## 1.0.1 and 1.0.0

- First releases: Stereo, Mono, Side, Left and Right monitoring; goniometer, phase correlation and mono fold-down loss; Simple and Advanced views; MIDI Learn and an on/off *Mono* parameter for DAW controller mapping; bit-exact Stereo and bypass.
